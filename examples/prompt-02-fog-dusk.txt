Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: portfolio preview for a painting-style image skill
Primary request: A quiet painting of one figure, seen from behind, standing alone on a low stone breakwater that runs out into a cold sea, coat collar up, hands in pockets, looking out. The face is never readable.
Style/medium: Original oil on canvas, matte and chalky, dry-brush and scumbled strokes. The figure's outline is not closed - its shoulders, coat hem and legs dissolve into the wet air and merge with the stone so that the body is a soft dark mass rather than a cut-out shape. Broad flat dragged paint in the water and sky, visible canvas weave, uneven finish, some areas wiped down and left raw. A painted memory, not a photograph.
Composition/framing: Portrait format, roughly 4:5. The figure is small, about one seventh of the image height, left of centre. Sky and sea occupy more than three quarters of the picture. Horizon slightly high. Unhurried, off-centre framing, as if someone glanced up and saw this.
Lighting/mood: Flat, cold sea fog at dusk. No visible sun and no sun glitter path. The light is a pale diffuse band low behind the fog bank, just bright enough to silhouette the figure. Quiet, distant, unresolved.
Color: chalk white, bone, grey-blue, olive, faint ochre and dusty rose, all muted and greyed. A single warm accent only - one small distant harbour lamp, no larger than a brush stroke. Everything else recedes into weather.
Constraints: face and identity withheld; exactly one figure, no crowd; no readable text, sign, logo, or signature; no clearly dated objects; completely original subject and composition.
Avoid: photographic defocus, bokeh, lens blur, radial or gaussian blur, tilt-shift, digital airbrushing, smooth gradients, sharp facial features, high saturation, golden sunset, postcard sunset palette, glittering highlight path on water, picturesque romantic vista, cinematic teal-and-orange grading, HDR, double-exposure ghosts, fantasy or horror atmosphere, sepia filter over a photo, glossy varnish, crowds, staged emotion.
