Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: series panel 1 of 6 - showcase
Primary request: A quiet painting of one traveller standing alone on a snow-covered rural station platform at dusk, seen from behind at the far end of the platform, coat collar up, a small bag at his feet. He is tiny in the frame, about one eighteenth of the image height. The face is never readable and the head is a soft dark shape.
Style/medium: Original oil on canvas, matte and chalky, dry-brush and scumbled strokes. The figure's outline is open - coat hem, legs and shoes dissolve into the snow and merge with it, so the body reads as a soft dark mass rather than a cut-out shape. Broad dragged paint, visible canvas weave, uneven finish - some passages resolved, others wiped down and left raw. A painted memory, not a photograph.
Composition/framing: Portrait 4:5. The platform runs from the lower left toward the middle distance; blank snowfield and pale sky occupy more than four fifths of the picture; horizon slightly high; the figure stands off-centre.
Lighting/mood: White fog and settling snow with almost no contrast; the light is diffuse and comes from behind the fog bank, just enough to silhouette him. Quiet, distant, unresolved - the feeling of a moment remembered years later.
Color: chalk white, bone, pale grey-blue and a faint olive, all muted and greyed; exactly one small warm accent in the whole image - a station lamp far down the platform, no larger than a brush stroke.
Constraints: face and identity withheld; exactly one figure, no crowd; no readable text, sign, timetable, logo or signature; no clearly dated objects; completely original subject and composition.
Avoid: photographic defocus, bokeh, lens blur, radial or gaussian blur, tilt-shift, digital airbrushing, smooth gradients, sharp facial features, high saturation, golden postcard sunset, cinematic teal-and-orange grading, HDR, double-exposure ghosts, fantasy or horror atmosphere, sepia filter over a photo, glossy varnish, crowds, staged emotion.
