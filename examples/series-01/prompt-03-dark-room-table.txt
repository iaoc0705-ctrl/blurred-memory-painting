Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: series panel 3 of 6 - showcase
Primary request: A quiet painting of one figure seated at a table in a dim room, seen from behind. Only the back and shoulders are visible, leaning slightly forward; a cup or an open book is suggested by a brush stroke. No face and no profile.
Style/medium: Original oil on canvas, matte and chalky, dry-brush and scumbled strokes. The figure's outline is open - the shoulders and the back of the head melt into the dark wall behind, so the body reads as a soft dark mass rather than a cut-out. Broad dragged paint, visible canvas weave, uneven finish - some passages resolved, others wiped down and left raw. A painted memory, not a photograph.
Composition/framing: Portrait 4:5. The room is mostly dark and empty; the figure is small, about one sixth of the image height, off-centre; a doorway or window on the far side holds the only light.
Lighting/mood: One warm lamp or lit window behind and beyond the figure, so the body is a dark mass against it; large areas of deep olive-green and grey-brown shadow. Quiet, distant, unresolved - the feeling of a moment remembered years later.
Color: olive, grey-brown, bone and grey-blue, all muted and greyed; exactly one small warm accent in the whole image - the lamp or the lit window.
Constraints: face and identity withheld; exactly one figure, no crowd; no readable text, sign, clock, logo or signature; no clearly dated objects; no table clutter; completely original subject and composition.
Avoid: photographic defocus, bokeh, lens blur, radial or gaussian blur, tilt-shift, digital airbrushing, smooth gradients, sharp facial features, high saturation, cinematic teal-and-orange grading, HDR, double-exposure ghosts, fantasy or horror atmosphere, sepia filter over a photo, glossy varnish, crowds, staged emotion.
