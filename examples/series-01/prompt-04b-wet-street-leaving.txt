Correction note: The first attempt failed in two dimensions only. (1) The coat came out bright saturated orange instead of muted rust. (2) The setting read as a lake shore rather than a wet street. Everything else about the composition was correct and must be preserved.
Primary request: A quiet painting of one figure stepping out of the picture at the very edge of the frame, on a wet empty street just after rain. Seen from behind and slightly from the side, mid-stride, only half of him still inside the frame, about one eighth of the image height so he is small. No face and no profile; the head is a soft dark shape that dissolves upward into the air.
Setting must be unambiguous: a straight empty urban street receding to a low horizon - a visible kerb line and gutter running away from the viewer, flat dark silhouettes of low buildings on both sides, no water, no shoreline, no lake, no beach, no hills. It is a road, not a seascape.
Style/medium: Original oil on canvas, matte and chalky, dry-brush and scumbled strokes. The figure's outline is open - the shoulder, coat hem and legs dissolve into the wet air and merge with the street, so the body reads as a soft dark mass rather than a cut-out. Broad dragged paint, visible canvas weave, uneven finish - some passages resolved, others wiped down and left raw. A painted memory, not a photograph.
Composition/framing: Portrait 4:5. The figure hugs the right edge; the rest is empty wet road under a pale sky; horizon low.
Lighting/mood: Flat backlit grey after rain. The brightest thing in the picture is one narrow pale band of reflection lying along the wet asphalt - narrow, not a broad shining sheet. Quiet, distant, unresolved - the feeling of a moment remembered years later.
Color: chalk white, bone, grey-blue and olive, all muted and greyed. Exactly one small warm accent in the whole image - his coat, drained almost to grey-rust and ochre. The coat must be muted and dusty, never orange, and must not glow against the grey.
Constraints: face and identity withheld; exactly one figure, no other people; remove all signage, shop fronts, brand marks, vehicles, street furniture and any legible text; no clearly dated objects; completely original subject and composition.
Avoid: saturated orange clothing, glowing warm coat, photographic defocus, bokeh, lens blur, radial or gaussian blur, tilt-shift, digital airbrushing, smooth gradients, sharp facial features, high saturation, neon reflections, cinematic teal-and-orange grading, HDR, double-exposure ghosts, fantasy or horror atmosphere, sepia filter over a photo, glossy varnish, crowds, staged emotion.
