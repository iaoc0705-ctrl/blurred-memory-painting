Style/medium: Original oil on canvas, matte and chalky, dry-brush and scumbled strokes, lost and blurred edges where the figure dissolves into the wet air, visible canvas weave, uneven finish - some passages resolved, others wiped down and left raw. A painted memory, not a photograph.

Constraints: face and identity withheld; exactly one figure, no crowd; no readable text, sign, logo, or signature; no clearly dated objects; completely original subject and composition.
